    How it works

    Three rules behind every video

    Safety first

    Some of these skills can hurt you. Every one that can has a safety note on its skill and at the start of its video. Learn the risky ones with someone qualified, follow your local rules for fires, fishing, drones and radio, and treat the money videos as education, not financial advice.

    The best video wins

    When someone has already made the definitive video on a topic, I don't remake it. The skill gets a short intro from me and a link to theirs, and it counts as unlocked.

    Interactive pages

    Many skills come with a page on this site that you can play with: drag the points, move the sliders, step through it at your own pace. The video walks through the same page.
