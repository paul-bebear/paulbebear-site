The energy question has a price tag

RTE, the grid operator, costed six ways to reach carbon neutrality. All six get there. The difference is cost: full system cost per year around 2060, against about €45bn today.

Government

Closest to N2 and N03: six EPR2 reactors confirmed and eight more under study, with solar targets cut in the final energy plan (PPE3, February 2026).

RN

Matches none of them. Every scenario, even N03, needs large renewables growth because new reactors cannot arrive before about 2037 while the existing fleet starts retiring in the 2040s. A wind and solar moratorium fails that test.

LFI

Closest to M0, the most demanding case: about €18bn a year more than N03, with large batteries, demand flexibility and build rates above the best European performances.

What isn't known yet

  • LFI's 2027 programme comes out on 6 November 2026. It may change the tax scale (70% or 90% top rate), the NATO exit timing and retirement at 60.
  • The RN platform is not final. Le Pen and Bardella disagree openly on pensions, and the financial-wealth tax has no published scale.
  • Le Pen has appealed to the Cour de cassation. If elected, presidential immunity would suspend the firm part of her sentence, so she may campaign under an electronic tag.
  • The 2027 budget may not pass. The finance committee rejected its revenue side 31 to 3 on 9 October. Moody's reviews France's rating on 23 October and S&P on 27 November.
  • No causal French study exists on whether the 1981 and 1997 regularisations attracted more migrants.

Caveats

  • The LFI costing is from 2024. Montaigne's page labels its figures as yearly but also describes the €233bn as spread over three years. Read the gap as an order of magnitude, somewhere between €60bn and €180bn a year.
  • Bond-market figures (the 10-year yield and the spread over German bonds, about 1.37 points on 16 October) come from market-data aggregators, not the Banque de France series.
  • Some RN details (the integrated-command exit, the financial-wealth tax threshold) come from candidate-comparison sites, not an official RN document.
  • The tax calculator is my own model of today's scale and LFI's amendment CF767. It is gross tax per share only.
  • Parties' own claims are shown as claims. Where no independent costing exists, the page says so rather than filling the gap.
